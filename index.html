Points Scoring Rules

Every event carries a fixed Max Pool Score. The total pool points are divided among all four houses based on their podium finish:

🥇 1st Place (Winner): 40% of Pool
🥈 2nd Place: 30% of Pool
🥉 3rd Place: 20% of Pool
🏅 4th Place: 10% of Pool

Example: A 50-point event awards 20, 15, 10, and 5 points respectively (Sum = 50).
